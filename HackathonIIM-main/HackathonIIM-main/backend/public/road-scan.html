Test steps

  1. Secure the phone and frame the surface, gap, and book.

  4. Move the book/copy into view or change the visible surface.
  5. Check whether the scan triggers a test alert.

Important: one ordinary camera cannot infer true height from these lines alone. This prototype detects image changes, which can also be caused by shadows, lighting, camera movement, or texture.
